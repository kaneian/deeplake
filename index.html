Midwater · the router

One request. Both sides settle together, or neither does.

  1. RequestThe bank names an amount, the dollar it holds and the dollar it wants.
  2. RankThe router prices every open channel and shows the all-in cost of each.
  3. ChooseThe bank picks its own partner, or lets Deep Lake take the cheapest route.
  4. SettleBoth legs lock in escrow and release in the same instant. If one fails, both return.
